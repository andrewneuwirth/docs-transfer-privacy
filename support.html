Docs Transfer Support

Docs Transfer moves reviewed text into a blank Google Doc in ordered chunks. It requires macOS 15 or later and Google Chrome.

If a PDF has no selectable text, paste its text into the app. If the app reports a mismatch, stop and inspect the Google Doc before starting a new run. The app never clears a nonblank document.

For help, open a support issue. Do not include private document text or sign-in details in a public issue.
