Docs Transfer Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026. Docs Transfer is a macOS app by Andrew Neuwirth.

What the app handles

You may paste text or select a PDF. The app extracts selectable PDF text locally, shows it for your review, and sends the reviewed text to the blank Google Doc URL you provide. It reads the document back to verify transfer accuracy.

Where data goes

Docs Transfer has no developer-operated server, account, analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting service. The developer does not receive your text, PDF, Google Docs URL, or Google credentials. The app creates a local text file during a transfer and removes it when the run ends normally. After a crash, that file may remain in the DocsTransfer folder in your Mac's Application Support directory until you delete it. A dedicated local Chrome profile keeps your Google sign-in so you do not need to sign in every time. Google Chrome and Google Docs handle your Google account, document, and network traffic under Google's own terms and privacy policy.

Your choices

You can stop a transfer at any time. To remove the dedicated browser's saved sign-in, delete the DocsTransfer folder in your Mac's Application Support directory after quitting the app. You can delete a Google Doc using your Google account.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when the app's data practices change. For privacy questions, use the support issue page.

Apple standard license agreement applies to the app.
